# Radhe Krishna Traders - Digital Marketing & Setup Blueprint

This blueprint outlines how to launch **Radhe Krishna Traders** on Google Maps/Search, set up reviews, and establish a digital marketing strategy across Google, Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp.

## 📍 Phase 1: Google Business Profile (Google Maps, Directions, & Ratings)
Establishing your presence on Google Maps is the single most important step for local B2B lead generation. When spice buyers search for *"red chilli wholesaler in Nagpur"*, your business card should appear.

### Step 1: Create the Listing
1. Go to **[google.com/business](https://www.google.com/business/)** and sign in with your business Gmail account (`radhakrishnaaa810@gmail.com`).
2. Click **Add business > Add single business**.
3. **Business Name**: `Radhe Krishna Traders`
4. **Business Category**: `Spice Wholesaler` or `Agricultural Service`
5. **Do you want to add a location customers can visit?**: Select **Yes**.
6. **Enter Address**: 
   - *Street Address*: `Plot no 48, Sidheshwar Nagar, Aradhana Nagar, Dighori`
   - *City*: `Nagpur`
   - *State*: `Maharashtra`
   - *PIN Code*: `440024`
7. Set your service area (e.g., *Nagpur, Maharashtra, All India*).
8. Add your contact phone: `+91 97303 12339` and your live website URL: `https://KakarotUltaInstict.github.io/radhe-krishna-traders/`.

### Step 2: Verification (Crucial)
Google will require you to verify that your business physically exists at this location.
* **Verification Method**: Typically, Google will request a **short video verification** of your office/yard (showing the street sign, your warehouse, and opening the door) or send a **physical postcard** with a verification code to Plot No. 48.
* Follow the prompts on the screen to complete this. It takes 3–5 business days to verify.

### Step 3: Set Up Directions & Ratings
Once verified, your business will appear on Google Maps.
* **Directions**: Customers can click "Directions" in search results, and Google Maps will navigate them straight to your address in Dighori.
* **Get Reviews/Ratings**: 
  1. In your Google Business Dashboard, click **Get more reviews**.
  2. Copy the **Short Review Link** (it looks like `g.page/radhe-krishna-traders/review`).
  3. Send this link on WhatsApp to every client you deal with. Reviews with 5-star ratings will push your listing to the top of Google Search.

## 📣 Phase 2: Google Search Ads (Targeting High-Value B2B Buyers)
Google Ads allows you to appear at the very top of Google Search when people search for terms like *"wholesale red chilli supplier"* or *"bulk Bhiwapur mirchi online"*.

### Suggested Keywords to Target:
* `nagpur red chilli wholesale`
* `bhiwapur red chilli suppliers`
* `buy red chilli powder bulk`
* `spice exporters maharashtra`
* `red chilli grade l1 price`

### Set Up Steps:
1. Go to **[ads.google.com](https://ads.google.com/)**.
2. Create a **Search Campaign** targeting India (or specific states like Gujarat, Rajasthan, and South India where spice demand is high).
3. Set your landing page to your live URL: `https://KakarotUltaInstict.github.io/radhe-krishna-traders/`.
4. Link the ad CTA directly to your WhatsApp enquiry form to capture phone numbers instantly.

## 📸 Phase 3: Facebook & Instagram Ads (Visual Brand Advertising)
Since red chillies are highly visual (especially the sun-drying farms and grinding process), social media ads are excellent for building trust.

### Setup Instructions:
1. Create a Facebook Business Page for **Radhe Krishna Traders** and link it to an Instagram business profile.
2. Go to **[adsmanager.facebook.com](https://adsmanager.facebook.com/)**.
3. Create a campaign with the **"Leads"** or **"Traffic"** objective, sending people to your WhatsApp.
4. **Ad Creatives**: Use the beautiful photos we generated of:
   - Your solar-drying farm yards under the Nagpur sun (`assets/hero_farm.jpg`).
   - The authentic stone spice grinding process (`assets/chilli_grinding.jpg`).
   - Clean product shots of L1, L2, L3 pods and powder.
5. **Audience Targeting**:
   - Target owners/managers of: *Spice mills, Pickle manufacturing companies, hotel chains, grocery delivery networks, and catering companies.*
