Direct Farm Sourcing

Partnering with Local Vidarbha Farmers

Nagpur is famous not just for oranges, but also for producing some of India's most intense, high-capsaicin red chillies, notably the famous **Bhiwapoori Mirchi**. The dry heat and volcanic red soils of the Vidarbha basin yield spices with deep color values and long-lasting pungency.

At Radha Krishna Traders, we establish direct partnerships with farmers in traditional chilli belts like Umred, Bhiwapur, and Katol, ensuring our buyers get completely unadulterated, pesticide-tested crops directly from the drying yards. We specialize in sourcing the authentic, high-quality **Bhiwapoori Mirchi** known for its bright red shade and perfect sharpness.

At Radha Krishna Traders, we preserve traditional techniques alongside modern hygiene standards. Unlike high-speed industrial mills that generate heat and destroy volatile capsaicin oils, we grind chillies at controlled cold temperatures.
